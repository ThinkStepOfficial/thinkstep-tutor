How to find a percentage of a number

"Percent" means out of 100. So 25% means 25 out of every 100. Once you know that, percentages get a lot easier.

The super-handy 10% trick

To find 10% of a number, divide it by 10 (move the decimal point one place left).

10% of 80 = 8

Now build other percentages from it: 20% is double that (16), 30% is three times (24), 5% is half of it (4).

Easy friends: 50%, 25% and 75%

  • 50% = half. 50% of 60 = 30.
  • 25% = a quarter, so halve it twice. 25% of 60 = 15.
  • 75% = 50% + 25%. 75% of 60 = 30 + 15 = 45.

Any percentage: the step-by-step way

  1. Turn the percent into a fraction out of 100: 15% = 15/100
  2. Multiply by the number: 15/100 × 80 = 1200/100
  3. Divide: 1200 ÷ 100 = 12. So 15% of 80 is 12.

Check with the 10% trick: 10% of 80 is 8, 5% is 4, and 8 + 4 = 12. ✓

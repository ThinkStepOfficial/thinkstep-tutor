Order of operations (PEMDAS), explained

Is 2 + 3 × 4 equal to 20 or 14? Math has a rule so everyone gets the same answer: the order of operations. (It's 14!)

PEMDAS

  1. Parentheses: do anything inside ( ) first.
  2. Exponents: powers like 3² next.
  3. Multiplication and Division: left to right, whichever comes first.
  4. Addition and Subtraction: left to right, whichever comes first.

A popular way to remember it: Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally. (Some places say BODMAS or BIDMAS. Same idea, different words.)

Example

(4 + 2) × 3² − 8 ÷ 2
  1. Parentheses: 4 + 2 = 6 → 6 × 3² − 8 ÷ 2
  2. Exponents: 3² = 9 → 6 × 9 − 8 ÷ 2
  3. Multiply and divide, left to right: 54 − 4
  4. Subtract: 50

The tricky part: multiplication doesn't always come before division. They're a team: do whichever comes first from left to right. Same for addition and subtraction. So 10 − 4 + 2 = 8, not 4.

Try one yourself

20 − 3 × (2 + 4) ÷ 9 = ?

Hint: start with the parentheses, then go left to right through × and ÷.

Common mistakes

  • Just working left to right without following PEMDAS.
  • Always multiplying before dividing, even when the division comes first.
  • Always adding before subtracting, even when the subtraction comes first.

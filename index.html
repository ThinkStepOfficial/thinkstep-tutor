About ThinkStep

ThinkStep started with a simple frustration: most AI tools just hand kids the answer, which feels helpful in the moment but doesn't actually teach anything.

So this was built the other way around — an AI tutor that acts like a patient teacher sitting next to you, nudging you toward the answer with small hints and encouraging questions, instead of doing the thinking for you.

It's a free personal project built around one idea: collect as little as possible and show no ads. Just a calm, friendly space to actually learn.

Questions parents and kids ask

Is ThinkStep really free?

Yes. ThinkStep is a 100% free AI homework helper. There are no ads and no payments, and you can start without signing up. A free account lets you keep going with no question limit.

Can I take a picture of my homework?

Yes. Tap the camera button, snap a photo of your worksheet or textbook page, and ThinkStep reads it, finds the exact problem you're stuck on, and puts a green box around it. Then it walks you through it step by step.

How does ThinkStep help me learn?

ThinkStep works like a patient tutor sitting next to you. It explains one small step at a time, asks you quick questions, and gives hints, so you understand how to solve the problem and can do the next one on your own.

What subjects and grades does it help with?

Any school subject for grades 1 to 12: math (fractions, long division, algebra, equations, geometry), science, reading, writing, grammar, history and more. Explanations match your grade, and you can make them easier or harder anytime.

Is ThinkStep safe for kids?

ThinkStep is built for kids. It stays on schoolwork, has safety rules for every conversation, shows no ads and never sells data. Kids under 13 need a parent's OK to make an account.

Do I need to download an app?

No. ThinkStep works in any web browser on a phone, tablet, Chromebook or computer. Just open the website and start asking.

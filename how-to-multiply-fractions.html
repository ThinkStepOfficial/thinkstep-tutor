How to multiply fractions, step by step

Good news: multiplying fractions is easier than adding them. You don't need matching bottom numbers. Just multiply straight across.

The rule

Multiply the top numbers together, then multiply the bottom numbers together.

2/3 × 4/5 = 2 × 4/3 × 5 = 8/15

Step by step

  1. Multiply the numerators (top numbers): 2 × 4 = 8.
  2. Multiply the denominators (bottom numbers): 3 × 5 = 15.
  3. Simplify if the top and bottom share a factor. 8 and 15 don't, so 8/15 is done.

A fraction times a whole number

Turn the whole number into a fraction by putting it over 1. So 3 × 2/7 becomes 3/1 × 2/7 = 6/7.

Mixed numbers

Change mixed numbers into improper fractions first. 1 1/2 = 3/2, because 1 whole is 2/2 and 2/2 + 1/2 = 3/2. Then multiply: 3/2 × 2/3 = 6/6 = 1.

Shortcut: you can simplify before you multiply. In 3/4 × 8/9, the 3 and 9 share a 3, and the 4 and 8 share a 4. Cross them down to 1/1 × 2/3 = 2/3. Smaller numbers, fewer mistakes.

Try one yourself

5/6 × 3/10 = ?

Hint: multiply across, then look for a number that divides both the top and bottom.

Common mistakes

  • Looking for a common denominator. That's for adding and subtracting, not multiplying.
  • Forgetting to turn mixed numbers into improper fractions first.
  • Not simplifying the final answer.

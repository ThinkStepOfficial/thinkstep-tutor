How to find slope and graph y = mx + b

Every straight line on a graph can be written as y = mx + b. Once you know what m and b mean, graphing is easy.

What m and b mean

  • m is the slope: how steep the line is.
  • b is the y-intercept: where the line crosses the y-axis.

Slope is rise over run

Slope = how far up (rise) ÷ how far across (run). Between the points (1, 2) and (3, 6):

m = 6 − 2/3 − 1 = 4/2 = 2

A positive slope goes up to the right. A negative slope goes down. A slope of 0 is flat.

Graphing y = 2x + 1

  1. Plot b. Put a dot at (0, 1) on the y-axis.
  2. Use the slope. m = 2 = 2/1: go up 2, right 1, and plot another dot at (1, 3).
  3. Draw a straight line through the dots.

Check: pick an x, like x = 2. y = 2(2) + 1 = 5. Is (2, 5) on your line? It should be!

Try one yourself

Find the slope between (0, 4) and (2, 0), then write the equation of the line.

Common mistakes

  • Putting run over rise instead of rise over run.
  • Subtracting the points in different orders on the top and bottom.
  • Plotting b on the x-axis instead of the y-axis.
